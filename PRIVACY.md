# Privacy policy

Canvas Download Sorter runs entirely inside your browser.

- It reads the course name from Canvas course pages you open, and the URL of
  downloads that start from Canvas, in order to pick a folder name.
- It stores course names, the folder names you choose and the automatic-naming
  setting in Chrome's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`), which Chrome
  may sync between your own signed-in browsers. Nothing is sent to the
  developer or to anyone else.
- It makes no network requests of its own and contains no analytics.

Questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/Glustrio/canvas-download-sorter/issues.
